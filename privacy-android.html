Android Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

This policy covers Handy PJC / HandyPJC (掌中投控) for Android, package com.PiscesStudio.HandyPJC, developed by Pisces Studio. Features described below, including Pro purchases, backups and camera scanning, apply where available in your installed version. For iPhone and iPad, see the iOS privacy policy.

Projector control and local data

No HandyPJC account is required. Projector names, network addresses and ports, groups, scenes, settings, equipment maintenance records and recent execution results are stored on your device. Pisces Studio does not operate a server to receive these configurations or your PJLink passwords.

The Android 3.0 app protects saved PJLink passwords using Android Keystore-backed encryption. It connects directly to the projector addresses you select using PJLink, including authentication when required. PJLink traffic is not encrypted by TLS; use a network you trust. Local network discovery runs when you request it. Equipment health records refer to projector status, not personal medical information.

Camera text recognition

Where available, you can use the system camera to capture an image for IP address recognition. ML Kit processes the image on the device. HandyPJC does not retain or upload the photo. The camera application may handle its own copies under its settings.

Google ML Kit can send technical information to Google for diagnostics and usage analysis, including app and device information, installation identifiers, performance, configuration and feature events. This is separate from the image processed on your device. See Google’s ML Kit data disclosure.

Advertising and privacy choices

The free app uses Google AdMob. Depending on your region, consent and settings, Google and its advertising partners process IP-derived approximate location, device or advertising identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. This is not a request for GPS location access.

The app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform for applicable consent choices and gates ad requests on the platform’s consent status. Where available, open Settings > Privacy choices to review your choices. Declining personalised advertising does not remove projector-control features. You can also manage advertising identifiers in Android settings. Versions supporting Pro stop requesting banner ads when Pro is active.

See Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps using its services. Google’s advertising and ML Kit SDKs use encrypted connections for their transmitted data.

Google Play purchases

Where Pro purchases are available, Google Play processes the payment. HandyPJC does not receive your card or bank details. The app uses the purchase status and a signed receipt to verify Pro, restore purchases and provide limited offline access. The Android 3.0 app caches verification for up to seven days and checks ownership again when it can connect. Google retains transaction records under its own policies.

Backups and exports

Where available, manual JSON backups contain projector network configurations, groups and scenes, but exclude passwords, purchase receipts and history. Equipment reports may also be exported. You choose the destination through Android’s document picker; a selected cloud provider handles uploaded files under its own policy. These exports do not provide automatic iCloud or cross-platform syncing. Delete exported copies separately when you no longer need them.

Retention, deletion and contact

You can remove saved projectors and their passwords in the app. Clear the app’s storage in Android settings or uninstall to remove local app data. The Android 3.0 app excludes its internal data from Android cloud backup and device transfer. Exports and Google’s purchase or advertising records are not removed by uninstalling.

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send and use it to respond and resolve your request. Do not send projector passwords or payment details. We keep support correspondence as needed for that purpose and applicable obligations. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information we hold; we cannot retrieve local projector data that was never sent to us. Google handles requests for data it controls under its own policies.

Children and policy updates

HandyPJC is an AV equipment utility and is not directed to children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has sent personal information to us. We will post policy changes here with an updated date. The previous Android policy remains available at its earlier address for reference.

投影機控制與本機資料

不需建立 HandyPJC 帳號。投影機名稱、網路位址與連接埠、群組、場景、設定、設備維護紀錄及近期執行結果儲存在您的裝置上。Pisces Studio 未營運接收這些設定或 PJLink 密碼的伺服器。

Android 3.0 版使用 Android Keystore 保護的加密方式保存 PJLink 密碼。App 透過 PJLink 直接連接您指定的投影機，並在需要時進行驗證。PJLink 通訊不使用 TLS 加密，請使用可信任的網路。區域網路搜尋由您主動啟動。設備健康紀錄指投影機狀態，不是個人醫療資訊。

相機文字辨識

若版本提供此功能，您可使用系統相機拍攝影像以辨識 IP 位址。ML Kit 在裝置上處理影像；HandyPJC 不保留或上傳照片。系統相機可能依其自身設定處理照片副本。

Google ML Kit 可能將技術資訊傳送至 Google，用於診斷與使用分析，包括 App 與裝置資訊、安裝識別碼、效能、設定與功能事件。這些資訊與在本機處理的影像不同。詳見 Google ML Kit 資料揭露說明。

廣告與隱私權選項

免費版使用 Google AdMob。依您的地區、同意與設定，Google 及其廣告合作夥伴會處理由 IP 推估的概略位置、裝置或廣告識別碼、App 與廣告互動及診斷資訊，用於廣告、成效評估與防止詐欺。這不代表 App 要求 GPS 定位權限。

App 使用 Google User Messaging Platform 提供適用的同意選項，並依該平台的同意狀態決定是否請求廣告。若相關選項適用，您可從「設定 > 隱私權選項」檢視選擇。拒絕個人化廣告不會移除投影機控制功能。您也可在 Android 設定管理廣告識別碼。支援 Pro 的版本在 Pro 生效時停止請求橫幅廣告。

詳見 Google 隱私權政策及 Google 如何使用採用其服務的 App 所提供的資訊。Google 廣告與 ML Kit SDK 傳送的資料使用加密連線。

Google Play 購買

若版本提供 Pro 購買，付款由 Google Play 處理，HandyPJC 不會取得您的信用卡或銀行資料。App 使用購買狀態與簽章收據驗證 Pro、恢復購買並提供有限的離線使用。Android 3.0 版最多快取七天驗證結果，並在可連線時重新確認購買權益。Google 依其政策保留交易紀錄。

備份與匯出

若版本提供此功能，手動 JSON 備份包含投影機網路設定、群組與場景，不包含密碼、購買收據或歷程。您也可匯出設備報告。儲存位置由您透過 Android 文件選擇器決定；若選擇雲端供應商，該供應商依其政策處理上傳檔案。這些匯出不提供自動 iCloud 或跨平台同步。不再需要時，請另行刪除匯出的副本。

保留、刪除與支援聯絡

您可在 App 中刪除投影機及其已存密碼。透過 Android 設定清除 App 儲存空間或解除安裝，可移除本機 App 資料。Android 3.0 版將內部資料排除於 Android 雲端備份與裝置移轉。解除安裝不會刪除匯出檔案，也不會刪除 Google 保存的購買或廣告紀錄。

若您寄信聯絡支援，我們會收到您的電子郵件地址及您主動提供的資訊，用於回覆並處理問題。請勿寄送投影機密碼或付款資料。我們依處理需求及適用義務保留支援信件。您可聯絡我們要求存取、更正或刪除我們持有的資料；我們無法擷取從未傳送給我們的本機投影機資料。Google 依其政策處理其控制之資料的相關請求。

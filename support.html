Android support

Download HandyPJC on Google Play. The Android app is also listed as Handy PJC (package: com.PiscesStudio.HandyPJC).

Connecting to a projector

Connect your Android device and projector to the same local network. Enable PJLink or network control on the projector and check its IP address and PJLink password. If discovery fails, check for guest Wi-Fi or client isolation and try entering the IP address if your app version offers manual setup.

Compatibility, features, and ads

Check Google Play for device compatibility and the features of your installed Android version. The iOS instructions below for Apple Watch, iCloud, and Pro purchases do not apply to Android. Advertising availability depends on the installed version.

Reporting an Android issue

Include your Android version, device model, HandyPJC version, projector model, and the steps that caused the problem. Please omit passwords and other private information.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is PJLink?

PJLink is a standard protocol for controlling projectors over a network. Many projectors from manufacturers such as Epson, Panasonic, NEC, and Sony support PJLink Class 1 or Class 2. HandyPJC supports both classes; available commands and maintenance information depend on the projector model.

My projector is not found during network scan

On the Home screen, tap ＋, then Network Scan. Make sure your iPhone or iPad and projector are on the same local network, Local Network permission is enabled for HandyPJC, client isolation is disabled on Wi-Fi, and PJLink or Network Control is enabled on the projector. You can also choose ＋ > Add Projector to enter the IP address manually.

I get an "Authentication Failed" error

Your projector requires a PJLink password. HandyPJC tries common default passwords automatically, but if your projector uses a custom password, you'll need to enter it manually. Go to the projector's edit screen and set the correct password.

How do I run a scene quickly?

Pin the scene in its editor. It will appear under Pinned Scenes on the iPhone or iPad Home screen and in the HandyPJC Apple Watch app. Tap the scene, review any confirmation, then choose Run. Keep the paired iPhone reachable while running a scene from Apple Watch.

Why does Apple Watch not show my latest scene?

Confirm the scene is pinned, then open HandyPJC on the iPhone once to refresh Watch data. Keep Bluetooth and Wi-Fi enabled and make sure the Watch app is installed. Apple Watch sends commands through the paired iPhone; it does not connect directly to projectors.

How do image blanking and audio mute work?

They are independent controls. Blank Image hides the projected image without powering the projector off; Restore Image shows it again. Mute Audio and Unmute Audio control projector audio when the model supports it.

What do Ready, Warning, Failed, and Unknown mean?

Ready means the projector responded normally. Warning indicates a reported device or maintenance warning. Failed means the check could not complete, for example because the device was offline or authentication failed. Unknown means there is no recent result. Tap the shield button on Home, then run Inspect All to refresh the venue.

Can I control projectors remotely (outside my network)?

HandyPJC communicates directly with projectors over your local area network. Controlling projectors from outside your network requires VPN access to the same network where your projectors are connected.

How do I delete all my data?

Delete projectors in HandyPJC first so their saved PJLink passwords are removed from Keychain, then uninstall the App to remove its local database and preferences. If iCloud sync is active, you can separately manage the App's private iCloud data from your Apple Account and iCloud storage settings.

How do I remove ads?

Purchase HandyPJC Pro from Settings. This one-time purchase removes ads and unlocks unlimited projectors, groups, scenes, Apple Watch, health checks, and maintenance tools. If you already purchased Pro, choose Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account.

System Requirements

  • iOS 17.0 or later
  • iPhone or iPad; Apple Watch features require watchOS 10.0 or later
  • Wi-Fi connection on the same network as your projectors
  • Projectors with PJLink Class 1 or Class 2 support

常見問題

什麼是 PJLink？

PJLink 是一種透過網路控制投影機的標準協定。Epson、Panasonic、NEC、Sony 等品牌的許多投影機支援 PJLink Class 1 或 Class 2。掌中投控支援兩種等級；可用指令及維護資訊仍依投影機型號而異。

網路掃描時找不到我的投影機

在首頁點擊「＋」，再選擇「網路掃描」。請確認 iPhone 或 iPad 與投影機位於同一個區域網路、已允許掌中投控使用「區域網路」、Wi-Fi 未啟用用戶端隔離，且投影機已開啟 PJLink 或網路控制。也可以選擇「＋ > 新增投影機」手動輸入 IP 位址。

出現「認證失敗」錯誤

您的投影機需要 PJLink 密碼。掌中投控會自動嘗試常見的預設密碼，但如果您的投影機使用了自訂密碼，則需要手動輸入。請進入投影機的編輯畫面並設定正確的密碼。

如何快速執行場景？

先在場景編輯頁將場景設為置頂，它便會出現在 iPhone／iPad 首頁的「置頂場景」及 Apple Watch App 中。點擊場景、確認提示後，再選擇「執行」。從 Apple Watch 執行時，請讓配對的 iPhone 保持可連線。

Apple Watch 為什麼沒有顯示最新場景？

請先確認場景已置頂，再開啟一次 iPhone 上的掌中投控以更新 Watch 資料。請保持藍牙與 Wi-Fi 開啟，並確認 Watch App 已完成安裝。Apple Watch 會透過配對的 iPhone 傳送指令，不會直接連接投影機。

關閉影像與聲音靜音有什麼不同？

兩者可以分別控制。「關閉影像」會隱藏投影畫面，但不會關閉投影機；使用「恢復影像」即可重新顯示。「靜音」與「取消靜音」則控制投影機聲音，實際效果依型號是否支援而定。

就緒、警告、失敗與未知代表什麼？

就緒表示投影機正常回應；警告表示設備或維護資訊出現警示；失敗表示巡檢未能完成，例如設備離線或認證失敗；未知表示沒有近期結果。點擊首頁的盾牌按鈕，再執行「巡檢全部」即可更新場地狀態。

能否從外部網路遠端控制投影機？

掌中投控透過區域網路直接與投影機通訊。若要從外部網路控制投影機，需要使用 VPN 連接到投影機所在的網路。

如何刪除所有資料？

請先在掌中投控中刪除投影機，使其儲存於鑰匙圈的 PJLink 密碼一併移除，再解除安裝 App 以刪除本機資料庫及偏好設定。若已啟用 iCloud 同步，您也可以另外從 Apple 帳號及 iCloud 儲存空間設定管理本 App 的私人 iCloud 資料。

如何移除廣告？

您可以在「設定」購買「掌中投控 Pro」。這是一次性購買，將移除廣告，並解鎖無限投影機、群組、場景、Apple Watch、健康巡檢及維護工具。若已購買過，請使用相同 Apple 帳號並點擊「恢復購買」。

  • iPhone 或 iPad；Apple Watch 功能需要 watchOS 10.0 或更新版本
  • 與投影機在同一網路的 Wi-Fi 連線
  • 支援 PJLink Class 1 或 Class 2 的投影機

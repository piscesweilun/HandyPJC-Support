Frequently Asked Questions

What is PJLink?

PJLink is a standard protocol for controlling projectors over a network. Most modern projectors from manufacturers like Epson, Panasonic, NEC, and Sony support PJLink Class 1. HandyPJC uses this protocol to communicate with your projectors.

My projector is not found during network scan

Make sure your iPhone/iPad and the projector are connected to the same Wi-Fi network. Also verify that PJLink is enabled in your projector's network settings. Some projectors require you to enable "PJLink" or "Network Control" in the settings menu.

I get an "Authentication Failed" error

Your projector requires a PJLink password. HandyPJC tries common default passwords automatically, but if your projector uses a custom password, you'll need to enter it manually. Go to the projector's edit screen and set the correct password.

Can I control projectors remotely (outside my network)?

HandyPJC communicates directly with projectors over your local area network. Controlling projectors from outside your network requires VPN access to the same network where your projectors are connected.

How do I delete all my data?

Simply uninstall the App from your device. All local data and Keychain entries will be removed. If iCloud sync is enabled, you can also clear iCloud data from Settings > [Your Name] > iCloud > Manage Storage.

How do I remove ads?

You can purchase "HandyPJC Pro" from the Settings menu. This one-time purchase removes all advertisements, unlocks unlimited projectors, and enables group management features.

System Requirements

  • iOS 17.0 or later
  • iPhone or iPad
  • Wi-Fi connection on the same network as your projectors
  • Projectors with PJLink Class 1 support

常見問題

什麼是 PJLink？

PJLink 是一種透過網路控制投影機的標準協定。大多數來自 Epson、Panasonic、NEC、Sony 等品牌的現代投影機都支援 PJLink Class 1。掌中投控使用此協定與您的投影機通訊。

網路掃描時找不到我的投影機

請確認您的 iPhone/iPad 與投影機連接在同一個 Wi-Fi 網路。同時確認投影機的網路設定中已啟用 PJLink。部分投影機需要您在設定選單中啟用「PJLink」或「網路控制」功能。

出現「認證失敗」錯誤

您的投影機需要 PJLink 密碼。掌中投控會自動嘗試常見的預設密碼，但如果您的投影機使用了自訂密碼，則需要手動輸入。請進入投影機的編輯畫面並設定正確的密碼。

能否從外部網路遠端控制投影機？

掌中投控透過區域網路直接與投影機通訊。若要從外部網路控制投影機，需要使用 VPN 連接到投影機所在的網路。

如何刪除所有資料？

只需從您的裝置解除安裝應用程式即可。所有本機資料和鑰匙圈項目都會被移除。若已啟用 iCloud 同步，您也可以從「設定」>「[您的名稱]」>「iCloud」>「管理儲存空間」清除 iCloud 資料。

如何移除廣告？

您可以在「設定」選單中購買「掌中投控 Pro」。這是一次性購買，將移除所有廣告、解鎖無限投影機，並啟用群組管理功能。

系統需求

  • iOS 17.0 或更新版本
  • iPhone 或 iPad
  • 與投影機在同一網路的 Wi-Fi 連線
  • 支援 PJLink Class 1 的投影機

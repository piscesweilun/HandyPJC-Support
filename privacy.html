Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 20, 2025

Overview

HandyPJC ("the App") is developed by Pisces Studio. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Information We Collect

HandyPJC is designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers.

The App stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Projector configurations — names, IP addresses, and port numbers of your projectors
  • Authentication credentials — PJLink passwords stored securely in the iOS Keychain
  • Group settings — projector group names and assignments
  • App preferences — such as scan timeout settings

iCloud Sync

If you enable iCloud sync, your projector configurations and group settings are synced across your devices using Apple's CloudKit. This data is stored in your private iCloud container and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not have access to your iCloud data.

Network Access

The App communicates with projectors on your local network using the PJLink protocol (TCP port 4352). This communication happens entirely within your local area network and does not pass through any external servers.

The App uses your device's camera solely for scanning IP addresses from labels or displays. Camera images are processed on-device and are never stored or transmitted.

Third-Party Services

HandyPJC uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob — We display advertisements via Google AdMob. Google may collect and use data to serve personalised ads. You can review Google's Privacy Policy for more details. You can opt out of personalised ads in your device settings.
  • Apple StoreKit (In-App Purchases) — We offer a "HandyPJC Pro" in-app purchase to unlock premium features and remove ads. Purchase transactions are processed entirely by Apple and governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Data Retention

All data is stored locally on your device and in your private iCloud container (if enabled). You can delete all App data at any time by uninstalling the App.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The App is a professional tool intended for use by AV professionals and facility managers.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

概述

掌中投控（HandyPJC，以下稱「本應用程式」）由 Pisces Studio 開發。我們致力於保護您的隱私。本隱私權政策說明我們如何收集、使用資訊，以及您的相關權利。

我們收集的資訊

掌中投控在設計上注重隱私保護。我們不會收集、儲存或傳輸任何個人資料至外部伺服器。

本應用程式僅在您的裝置本機儲存以下資料：

  • 投影機設定 — 投影機的名稱、IP 位址及連接埠號
  • 認證資訊 — PJLink 密碼安全儲存於 iOS 鑰匙圈（Keychain）
  • 群組設定 — 投影機群組的名稱及分配
  • 應用程式偏好設定 — 例如掃描逾時等設定

iCloud 同步

若您啟用 iCloud 同步功能，投影機設定與群組設定將透過 Apple 的 CloudKit 在您的裝置間同步。這些資料儲存在您的私人 iCloud 容器中，受 Apple 隱私權政策的規範。我們無法存取您的 iCloud 資料。

網路存取

本應用程式透過 PJLink 協定（TCP 連接埠 4352）與您區域網路中的投影機通訊。所有通訊均在您的區域網路內進行，不會經過任何外部伺服器。

本應用程式使用您裝置的相機僅用於掃描標籤或螢幕上的 IP 位址。相機影像在裝置上處理，不會被儲存或傳輸。

第三方服務

掌中投控使用以下第三方服務：

  • Google AdMob — 我們透過 Google AdMob 顯示廣告。Google 可能會收集和使用資料以提供個人化廣告。詳情請參閱 Google 隱私權政策。您可以在裝置設定中關閉個人化廣告。
  • Apple StoreKit（應用程式內購買） — 我們提供「掌中投控 Pro」應用程式內購買以解鎖進階功能並移除廣告。購買交易完全由 Apple 處理，受 Apple 隱私權政策規範。

資料保留

所有資料均儲存在您的裝置本機及您的私人 iCloud 容器中（若已啟用）。您可以隨時透過解除安裝應用程式來刪除所有應用程式資料。

兒童隱私

本應用程式不會故意收集 13 歲以下兒童的資訊。本應用程式是專為視聽專業人員及設施管理人員設計的專業工具。

政策變更

我們可能會不時更新本隱私權政策。任何變更將在本頁面公布，並更新修訂日期。

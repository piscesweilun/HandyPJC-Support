HandyPJC Android: connected-device foreground service

Google Play review demonstration recorded on an Android phone using a PJLink simulator with delayed responses. This is the actual app UI; clips are trimmed and notification views cropped to exclude unrelated notifications.

Open or download the MP4 video

  1. The user starts a projector status inspection with “巡檢全部” (Inspect all).
  2. The ongoing notification displays “執行中…” (Running) and “停止” (Stop).
  3. After Stop is requested, the notification displays “已取消” (Cancelled).

The app uses FOREGROUND_SERVICE_CONNECTED_DEVICE for user-initiated PJLink communication over the local network during inspections and multi-step projector scenes. It shows progress with a Stop action and ends the service on completion or cancellation. Interrupting the work may leave an inspection incomplete or a scene partially executed. Hardware commands are not automatically restarted.

This clip demonstrates the inspection workflow. It does not demonstrate a Pro scene run.
